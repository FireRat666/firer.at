FireRat's FireScreen Guide

Click & Drag to Scroll
VR users point and pull the trigger to click

The browser is local: everyone has their own screen.
You can't see other people's pages, and logins stay on your device.

Moving the Screen

Grab the pill under the screen and carry it where you want it.
Let go and it stays put. Bumping into the screen won't push it.

Top Row


Go back and forward between pages


Go to the screen's home page


Mute, turn the volume down, turn the volume up

Left Side


Opens this page


Opens Google


Hides the other buttons (click again to bring them back)


Opens a keyboard for typing into the page


Turns the screen to always face you

Right Side


Make the screen bigger or smaller

Some screens have extra text buttons on the right that the space owner set up, like quick links to sites.

Hand Controls

Some spaces add small buttons on your hand for volume, mute, home,
and to lock yourself in place (red when locked).
